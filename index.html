Layer 02 · Two languages

Rewritten, not translated.

Many of the drivers we serve in Texas and New Mexico think in Spanish first. The Spanish form isn't a translation layer. It's a second file, written side by side with the English one, by someone who lives in both languages.

Layer 03 · Logic

The form thinks so they don't have to.

Layer 07 · Release discipline

Twenty-one versions, zero surprises.

The form lives in a repository shared with Inaza. Every change bumps the version and adds a changelog entry, or the build stops. A test page gets every release first. Only then does it go live.

Layer 10 · The decision

The deepest layer is a no.

We didn't start with a form. We started with an AI voice agent. I tested it against 14 real-call scenarios, compared two versions, and recommended we not launch it.

Saying no to the shiny thing is what made room for the simple one.

Recommendation No-go
