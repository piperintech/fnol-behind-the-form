# Everything behind a simple form

A scroll-through case study by **Manuela Gonzalez Piper**: the ten layers behind SNAP Insurance's one-page, bilingual claim form, from the front door to the decision not to launch an AI voice agent.

**Live:** https://piperintech.github.io/fnol-behind-the-form/

Built at SNAP Insurance together with vendor partner Inaza. Every screen and number on the page is recreated for illustration; no real customer information is shown.
